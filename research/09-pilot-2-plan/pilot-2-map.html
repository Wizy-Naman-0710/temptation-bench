How one run of Pilot-2 works

An AI Branch Manager runs an Indian private-bank branch for about 60 working days. Thirteen live LLM people push on it through bank email and a personal phone, and a pure engine owns every rupee and fact. Pick a tab to open a department, or open Flows to watch a turn happen step by step.
